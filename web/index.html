Workflow

How it works

1 · Calibrate

Flash the calibration firmware, zero each sensor. Constants are saved on the device.

2 · Collect

Flash the collection firmware, capture pure and spiked samples. The CSV is written in your browser.

3 · Train

Run the Python trainer on that CSV. It exports model.h + scaler.h and an honest report.

4 · Deploy

Flash the deployment firmware. The device tests, decides and flushes on its own.

If the board is not detected

Most DevKit boards enter the bootloader on their own. If yours does not: hold BOOT, tap EN/RST, release BOOT, then pick the port again. A data-capable USB cable matters — charge-only cables show no port at all.

Run

The finished tester is standalone. It serves its own dashboard, so you open that instead of this page.

Three steps

  1. Power the device. The TFT shows its Wi-Fi name and password.
  2. Join AquaMilk-XXXX from your phone or laptop (XXXX = the last two bytes of its MAC).
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1 — verdict, live sensors, history, settings.

Told it to join your own Wi-Fi in Settings? Then it also answers at http://aquamilk.local (mDNS). The pad alone works with no phone at all: tap to test, double tap to flush, long-press for the menu.

Why the dashboard is not embedded here

This page is served over HTTPS and the device speaks plain HTTP on your local network. Browsers block that mix, and an ESP32 cannot hold a trusted certificate for a private IP. So the dashboard ships on the device — same design language, no internet required.
